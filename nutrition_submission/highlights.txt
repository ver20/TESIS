Highlights

- LightGBM predicted childhood stunting in Peru with an AUC-ROC of 0.83
- Model trained on 304,271 children from 19 years of ENDES survey data
- Poverty, birth weight, size at birth and altitude drove predicted risk
- SHAP profiles show that risk factors differ across Peru's departments
- A web tool flags high-risk children for triage; experts rated it useful
