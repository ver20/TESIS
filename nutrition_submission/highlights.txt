Highlights

- LightGBM predicted childhood stunting in Peru with an AUC-ROC of 0.83
- The model used 304,271 children from 19 years of the ENDES survey
- Poverty, birth weight, size at birth and altitude were the top predictors
- The weight of each risk factor differs across Peru's 25 departments
- A web tool flags high-risk children for triage; experts rated it useful
